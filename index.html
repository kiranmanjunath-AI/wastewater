Disease Surveillance

Your wastewater is an early warning system for outbreaks — before most people know they're sick.

Every day, viruses shed by infected people flow into the sewer system. Scientists can detect them in water samples days before cases show up in clinics or hospitals — giving communities a head start on what's coming.
